SchoolPass Assessment - Task 3 Evidence: Screen Reader Announcement Transcripts
Assistive Technology: Windows Narrator (Manual Human Verification) / Chromium WAI-ARIA 1.2 Live Engine
Target Application: SchoolPass Parent Registration Form (/auth?view=register)
Audit Date: 2026-11-01
Auditors: Quality Assurance Team & Human Manual Accessibility Tester

================================================================================
EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & EVALUATION METHODOLOGY
================================================================================
This document records the auditory accessibility evaluation of the SchoolPass Parent
Registration form, contrasting the baseline implementation against the remediated
WAI-ARIA 1.2 compliant interface.

EVALUATION METHODOLOGY & AUDIT TRAIL:
1. Genuine Human Manual Testing (Windows Narrator):
   - Executed live on Windows 11 using the built-in Windows Narrator screen reader.
   - Navigated exclusively via keyboard (Tab, Shift+Tab, Arrow keys, Space, Enter).
   - Records high-level qualitative observations of spoken announcements. Exact spoken
     phrasing was not audio-recorded, so these are explicitly recorded as genuine
     human observations rather than a verbatim audio transcript.
2. WAI-ARIA Specification & Engine Reference Transcripts:
   - Field-by-field expected speech synthesis derived from Chromium Accessibility Tree
     (AOM) and MSAA/UIA mapping specifications.
   - The pre-remediation baseline represents structural DOM/ARIA analysis and engine
     specifications, as the historical baseline was not tested with Narrator.

================================================================================
SECTION I: GENUINE MANUAL WINDOWS NARRATOR OBSERVATIONS
================================================================================
Environment: Windows 11 / Windows Narrator (Built-in Screen Reader)
Tester: Human Manual Tester
Test Date: 2026-11-01
Status: VERIFIED & COMPLETE

Note on Recording: Exact spoken wording was not recorded to audio media. The
following are genuine, unembellished observations captured during live keyboard
navigation through the remediated form:

1. Keyboard Navigation & Labeling:
   - Narrator clearly announced form buttons and text labels as the tester navigated
     sequentially through the form controls using Tab and arrow keys.
   - Interactive elements (including input fields, password visibility toggles, legal
     review triggers, and the primary action button) received clear, unambiguous focus.

2. Inline Errors & Field Requirements:
   - Narrator announced field validation errors and outstanding required fields as the
     tester completed and blurred form fields.
   - Visual error messages and required indicators were matched by auditory cues,
     preventing silent validation failures.

3. Premature Submit Prevention & Requirement Guidance:
   - When the tester attempted to submit the form with required fields incomplete
     (e.g., terms checkbox unselected), Narrator actively communicated the outstanding
     requirements.
   - The form did not fail silently; instead, Narrator conveyed the missing condition
     and focus was directed to the unresolved requirement.

================================================================================
SECTION II: FIELD-BY-FIELD AUDIT TRANSCRIPTS (WAI-ARIA / UIA REFERENCE)
================================================================================

1. FULL NAME FIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Remediation:
- On Focus: "Full Name, edit, required, type in text."
- On Empty Blur: [SILENCE]. Visual text appears below input, but screen reader does
  not announce the error because the container lacked `role="alert"` and `aria-describedby`.
- On Invalid Name ('A'): [SILENCE]. Visual text "Please enter your first and last name"
  is unannounced.

After Remediation:
- On Focus: "Full Name, edit, required, placeholder e.g. Chinedu Okafor, type in text."
- On Empty Blur: "Full Name, edit, required, invalid entry. Alert: Enter your full name."
- On Invalid Name ('A') Blur: "Full Name, edit, required, invalid entry. Alert: Enter your first and last name. Each name must contain at least 2 characters."
- Rationale: Linked via `aria-invalid="true"`, `aria-describedby="fullName-error"`, and `role="alert" aria-live="polite"`.


2. EMAIL ADDRESS FIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Remediation:
- On Focus: "Email Address, edit, required, type in text."
- On Invalid Typing: [SILENCE]. No live feedback during typing.
- On Blur: Error appears visually without immediate speech interruption.

After Remediation:
- On Focus: "Email Address, edit, required, placeholder e.g. parent@example.com, type in text."
- On Invalid Typing ('invalid-email'): "Alert: Please enter a valid email address."
  (Politely announces formatting requirements dynamically during real-time signup).
- On Blur: "Email Address, edit, required, invalid entry. Alert: Please enter a valid email address."


3. STUDENT NUMBER FIELD (ADMISSION IDENTIFIER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Remediation:
- On Focus: "Student Number, edit, required, placeholder e.g. SP-2026-0001, type in text."
- Flaw Identified: The placeholder leaked the sensitive structural format of student IDs
  (Prefix: SP, Year: 2026, Delimiter: hyphen, Sequence: 4 digits).

After Remediation:
- On Focus: "Student Number, edit, required, placeholder Enter student number from admission letter, type in text."
- Benefit: Neutral, instructional placeholder prevents format enumeration attacks
  while guiding parents to their physical documentation.


4. PASSWORD FIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Remediation:
- On Focus: "Password, password edit, required, type in text."
- Requirements Announcement: [SILENCE]. Password complexity rules ("Minimum 8 characters")
  were rendered as visual text without `aria-describedby` association.
- On Insecure/Pwned Blur: Visual error text appeared below input without `role="alert"`;
  screen reader remained silent upon blur.
- Visibility Toggle: Lacked descriptive `aria-label`, `aria-controls`, and `aria-pressed`.

After Remediation:
- On Focus: "Password, password edit, required, Minimum 8 characters., type in text."
  (Associated via `aria-describedby="password-helper"`).
- On Short Password Blur (< 8 chars): "Password, password edit, required, invalid entry. Alert: Password must be at least 8 characters."
- On Weak/Common Password Blur: "Password, password edit, required, invalid entry. Alert: Choose a stronger password."
- Visibility Toggle: Focusable button announces: "Show password, button, not pressed, controls password".
  Activating button announces: "Hide password, button, pressed", toggling input to plain text.


5. CONFIRM PASSWORD FIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Remediation:
- On Focus: "Confirm Password, password edit, required, type in text."
- On Mismatch Blur: [SILENCE]. "Passwords do not match" appeared as unlinked visual text.

After Remediation:
- On Focus: "Confirm Password, password edit, required, type in text."
- On Mismatch Blur: "Confirm Password, password edit, required, invalid entry. Alert: Passwords do not match."
- Rationale: Linked via `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby="confirmPassword-error"`.


6. TERMS OF SERVICE & PRIVACY POLICY CHECKBOX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Remediation:
- On Focus: "I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, checkbox, unchecked."
- Flaw Identified: Checkbox lacked `aria-required="true"`. Screen reader user had no
  auditory indicator that this legal agreement was mandatory for registration.

After Remediation:
- On Focus: "I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, checkbox, required, unchecked."
- In Context: Contains interactive keyboard-accessible buttons for reviewing modal text
  of both legal documents without navigating away from the form.


7. SUBMIT BUTTON (PRIMARY ACTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Remediation (NATIVE DISABLED PATTERN):
- During Tab Navigation: [SKIPPED ENTIRELY]. Because the button was `<button disabled>`,
  the browser dropped it from the accessibility tab order. A keyboard or screen reader
  user navigating via Tab moved from the Terms checkbox directly out of the form,
  falsely concluding that the form had no submit button.
- If Reached via Arrow Keys: "Create Account, button, unavailable."
- On Click / Space / Enter: [COMPLETE SILENCE]. The browser drops all click events on
  disabled elements. The user received zero explanation of what was missing.

After Remediation (ACCESSIBLE ARIA-DISABLED PATTERN):
- During Tab Navigation: [FOCUSED IN NATURAL ORDER].
  Announcement: "Create Account, button, dimmed." (or "disabled" depending on reader).
- Visual State: Button displays distinct focus ring (2px solid primary outline, 2px offset)
  meeting WCAG 2.4.7 and WCAG 1.4.11 contrast requirements.
- On Click / Space / Enter Activation:
  Live Announcement: "Status: Please accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before creating your account."
  Programmatic Focus: Focus immediately shifts back to the terms checkbox:
  "I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, checkbox, required, unchecked."
