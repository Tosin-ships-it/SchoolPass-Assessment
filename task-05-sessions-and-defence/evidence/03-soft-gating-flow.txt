1. FLOW SPECIFICATION:
In the soft gating alternative model, registration immediately issues an authenticated
session cookie (schoolpass_session). The user bypasses the email verification gate and
enters the portal directly (/records). However, an accessible warning banner is displayed
on the layout, and high-risk mutation endpoints (such as submitting student number link
requests) enforce a strict server-side barrier (HTTP 403 Forbidden).
