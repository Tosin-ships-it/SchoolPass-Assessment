4. ARCHITECTURAL CONCLUSION & DEFENSE IN DEPTH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. IP-based rate limiting is a network-layer heuristic, NOT a complete identity control.
2. Legitimate IP rotation (cellular network re-assignment, mobile tethering, or VPN change)
   circumvents IP counters because the server cannot distinguish a rotating client from a new user.
3. Defense-in-Depth Required for Serious Applications:
   - Keying rate limits on Account Identity (e.g. parent email or phone number).
   - Exponential backoff per account (regardless of source IP).
   - Breached-password screening (HIBP k-anonymity) to neutralize credential stuffing.
   - Cryptographic proof of work or behavioral CAPTCHA escalation on suspicious anomalies.
